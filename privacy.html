01 Introduction & Core Principles

Welcome to Tebri ("we", "our", or "the app"), a Mid-Century Modern personal reading tracker, bookshelf organizer, and reading habit companion available as a native Android application and web application.

We believe your reading habits, thoughts, notes, and personal book collections are intimate and private. Tebri is engineered from the ground up on an offline-first architectural foundation. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertisements, and we do not profile your reading tastes.

02 Information We Collect and Process

A. Reading Library Data (On-Device)

When you use Tebri, you create and manage reading records including:

  • Books you add (titles, authors, ISBNs, page counts, genres, descriptions, publication years, and cover artwork URLs).
  • Reading statuses (Want to Read / TBR, Currently Reading, Finished, Did Not Finish / DNF).
  • Reading logs (pages read per session, dates, timestamps, session duration).
  • Personal notes, highlighted quotes, thoughts, star ratings, and written reviews.
  • Annual reading targets and daily page goals.

Storage: All of this data is stored locally on your device in an isolated, private Room/SQLite database (Android) or IndexedDB (Web). It never leaves your device unless you explicitly opt in to Google Drive synchronization or manually export a JSON backup file.

B. Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

Tebri includes an integrated barcode scanner to quickly look up books by their physical ISBN barcode.

  • The camera is activated only when you open the Barcode Scanner screen.
  • Frames are processed transiently in real time using the embedded on-device ZXing recognition engine solely to detect numeric ISBN barcodes.
  • No photos or videos are ever captured, saved to disk, or transmitted over any network.

C. Microphone & Audio

The production release version of Tebri on Google Play does not request, access, or utilize microphone permissions (RECORD_AUDIO).

D. Media & Device Storage

When you export or import a library backup (JSON format) or import a Goodreads CSV reading history, Tebri utilizes standard Android system file pickers (Storage Access Framework) to read or save only the specific file you choose. Tebri includes .nomedia files in its application directories to ensure internal databases and caches are never indexed or synced to Google Photos or cloud gallery apps.

03 Optional Google Services & Cloud Sync

Tebri provides optional cross-device synchronization between your Android devices and the Tebri Web companion.

Direct Client-to-Drive Architecture: Tebri operates with no proprietary intermediary server. Synchronization occurs directly between your device and Google's servers using your authenticated Google account.
  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to enable cloud sync, you sign in with your Google Account using official Google Identity Services / Play Services. We receive basic profile information (display name, email address, profile photo URL) solely to render your signed-in state in Settings. We never receive or store your Google password.
  • Google Drive AppData Scope (drive.appdata): Tebri requests access strictly and exclusively to your private Google Drive Application Data folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).
  • Restricted Access: Tebri can only read and write its own configuration file (tebri_cloud_sync.json). It has zero access to your personal Google Drive documents, photos, spreadsheets, or any other files.
  • The developer of Tebri has no access to your Google account or your cloud sync file.

04 External Book Metadata APIs

To enrich book records with cover art, author names, page counts, and descriptions, Tebri makes requests to the following public book catalog services:

  • Google Books API: Used to search for titles or lookup ISBN numbers. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Open Library API: Operated by the Internet Archive, used as an open-access book catalog fallback. Governed by the Internet Archive Privacy Policy.

These requests contain only the book search query or ISBN number. No personal user identifiers, account data, or reading logs are transmitted in these requests.

05 Zero Advertising, Tracking, or Analytics

No Ads. No Trackers. No Data Brokers.
Tebri contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero user tracking libraries, and zero behavioral analytics platforms (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no data aggregators).

06 Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

You have absolute sovereignty and complete control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or delete any individual book, reading log entry, quote, or note directly within the app at any time.
  • Local Data Wipe: You can completely and permanently delete all local data by choosing Clear Storage / Clear Data in your device's Android App Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Cloud Sync Disconnection & Deletion: You can disconnect your Google account at any time in Tebri's Settings screen. You can also permanently revoke Tebri's access and remove the app data file via your Google Account Management page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Data Portability: You can export your full library, logs, and settings to an unencrypted, human-readable JSON backup file at any time via Settings → Export JSON.

07 Security

All network communications with external services (Google Books API, Open Library, Google Drive API, Google Sign-In) are conducted strictly over encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS). Local data is protected within Android's sandboxed application container.

08 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Tebri does not target children under the age of 13, nor do we knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.
